SampleDrum

A sample-based drum instrument: an eight-by-four grid of cells, each holding a .wav you drop on it from the Finder. Each column is a drum on its own MIDI note (General MIDI by default); each row is a velocity band, so the harder the hit, the lower the row that plays.

This installs SampleDrum for every user on this Mac:

  • VST3 — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

You choose which on the next-but-one pane. Installing over an existing copy replaces it; your projects and the samples in them are untouched.

Quit your DAW before installing. A host that is already running holds the old plug-in open and will not see the new one until it is restarted.
