Installed

Open SampleDrum and choose a kit from your host's preset menu or browser: in Logic, the preset pop-up at the top of the plug-in window; in a VST3 host, its preset browser, under AE Cobley › SampleDrum.

Loading a kit replaces the whole grid — samples, levels and notes — so save a kit of your own as a preset first if you want to keep it.

To remove the kits: sudo rm -rf "/Library/Audio/Presets/AE Cobley/SampleDrum"
