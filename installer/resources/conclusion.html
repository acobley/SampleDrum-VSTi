Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. SampleDrum appears as an instrument by AE Cobley. Drop a .wav on a cell to load it; click the cell to hear it.

Three things worth knowing:

  • Samples play at their own level, and hits add up. Two full-scale samples struck together reach +6 dBFS. If a kit distorts, turn Output Trim down (−6 dB covers two coincident hits) rather than looking for a fault.
  • Velocity chooses the row, not the loudness. Each column’s four cells are four velocity bands; the level knob under a cell sets how loud it plays.
  • Your samples travel with the project. Every file you drop is saved inside the project, so it opens on another Mac without them — and project files grow by the size of the samples.
