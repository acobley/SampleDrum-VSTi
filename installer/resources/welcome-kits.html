SampleDrum Factory Kits

Ready-made drum kits for SampleDrum: every cell of the grid filled, with the samples inside each kit, so they play on any Mac and travel with your projects.

This installs the kits for every user on this Mac, into

  • /Library/Audio/Presets/AE Cobley/SampleDrum

where both the VST3 and the Audio Unit look for presets. It does not install SampleDrum itself — that is its own installer, and it needs to be installed too.

About 900 MB. Installing over an earlier set replaces kits of the same name; anything you saved there yourself is left alone.
